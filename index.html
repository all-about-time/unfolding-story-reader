About RENEW

Read slowly. Emphasise one word at a time. Notice what catches your attention. Explore what may sit beneath that response. Respond to what God is inviting.

RENEW is a reflective practice, not a way to force Scripture to say whatever we want. The later guided steps will retain this distinction.
